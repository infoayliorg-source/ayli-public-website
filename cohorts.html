THE AYLI COHORT MODEL

Learning Together.
Leading Together.

AYLI organizes participants into leadership cohorts that provide a structured environment for academic learning, personal development, professional growth and leadership engagement.

Each cohort brings together participants from different backgrounds and communities, creating opportunities for collaboration, dialogue, networking and shared learning.

THE COHORT JOURNEY

From Orientation to Graduation

AYLI's cohort experience follows a structured leadership development journey designed to guide participants through learning, assessment, presentation and graduation.

01

Application

Prospective participants apply to join the AYLI Leadership School and begin their leadership development journey.

02

Orientation

Participants receive program information, academic guidance and an introduction to the cohort experience.

03

Learning

Students engage in structured courses, lectures, discussions and leadership activities.

04

Assessment

Participants complete academic assessments and demonstrate their understanding of the subjects covered.

05

Presentation

Students have opportunities to present their work and demonstrate communication and leadership skills.

06

Graduation

Successful participants complete their cohort journey and receive recognition through the AYLI graduation process.

THE AYLI EXPERIENCE

A Community of Emerging Leaders

Cohorts are more than academic groups. They create communities where young people can exchange ideas, build friendships, develop confidence and discover opportunities to contribute to their communities.

Through this experience, AYLI seeks to strengthen both individual leadership capacity and a broader culture of responsible youth leadership.
