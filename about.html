The Africa Youth Leadership Institute is a youth leadership development institution committed to equipping young Africans with the knowledge, skills, character and opportunities needed to lead with purpose.

WHO WE ARE

A Leadership Institute for Africa's Next Generation

The Africa Youth Leadership Institute (AYLI) is a youth leadership development institution dedicated to preparing young people to become responsible, ethical and visionary leaders.

Through leadership education, professional development, civic engagement, mentorship and continental networking, AYLI creates opportunities for young people to discover their potential and develop the capacity to serve their communities and contribute to Africa's development.

The Institute operates through a central leadership structure headquartered in the Republic of Liberia, with national chapters and leadership representation across Africa.

01

OUR MISSION

Empowering Young Leaders

To equip young people across Africa with leadership knowledge, practical skills, ethical values and opportunities that enable them to become responsible leaders and positive contributors to their communities and societies.

02

OUR VISION

A Continent Led by Prepared Youth

To contribute to an Africa where young people are empowered, informed and prepared to provide visionary, ethical and transformative leadership.

OUR PURPOSE

Why AYLI Exists

Africa's young people represent a significant source of leadership, innovation and transformation. AYLI exists to help develop that potential through structured leadership education and meaningful opportunities for service and impact.

01

Build leadership capacity, confidence, knowledge and professional skills among young people.

03

04

OUR LEADERSHIP PHILOSOPHY

Leadership Is a Responsibility

AYLI believes that leadership is not simply a position or title. It is a responsibility to serve, influence positively, develop others and contribute to the advancement of society.

Our programs therefore seek to develop leaders who combine knowledge with character, confidence with humility and ambition with a commitment to service.

OUR CONTINENTAL REACH

From Liberia to Africa

AYLI is headquartered in the Republic of Liberia and is building a growing continental network of young leaders, national chapters and institutional partners.
