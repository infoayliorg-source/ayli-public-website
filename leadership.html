The Africa Youth Leadership Institute is guided by a dedicated leadership structure committed to developing responsible, ethical and visionary young leaders across Africa.

OUR LEADERSHIP

A Structure Built for Impact

AYLI operates through a coordinated leadership structure that connects the Executive Office, Continental Secretariat, professional directorates and national chapters.

Together, these offices provide strategic direction, academic leadership, student support, program coordination, admissions, communications and national representation.
