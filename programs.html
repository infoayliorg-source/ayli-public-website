AYLI PROGRAMS

Learn. Lead.
Serve. Transform.

AYLI programs are designed to equip young people with the knowledge, skills, values and experiences required to become effective leaders and positive contributors to Africa's future.

WHAT WE DO

Developing Leadership Beyond the Classroom

The Africa Youth Leadership Institute provides structured learning and development opportunities that help young people strengthen their leadership capacity and prepare for greater responsibility.

Our approach combines academic learning, practical leadership development, civic engagement, professional development and opportunities for young leaders to connect across Africa.
