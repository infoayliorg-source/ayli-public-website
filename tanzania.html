ABOUT THE CHAPTER

Building a New Generation of Leaders in Tanzania

The Africa Youth Leadership Institute Tanzania Chapter serves as a national platform for empowering young people with the knowledge, skills and values required to contribute meaningfully to their communities, nation and continent.

01

Leadership Development

Equipping young people with leadership knowledge, practical skills, confidence and ethical values.

02

Youth Engagement

Creating opportunities for Tanzanian youth to participate in meaningful initiatives, discussions and community activities.

03

National Impact

Supporting initiatives that encourage responsible citizenship, innovation, collaboration and sustainable community development.

OUR WORK

Advancing Youth Leadership

AYLI Tanzania focuses on creating pathways for young people to learn, lead, serve and create positive change.

01

Leadership Training

Leadership education and professional development designed to prepare young people for greater responsibilities.

02

Civic Engagement

Encouraging young people to understand their responsibilities and participate constructively in society.

03

Supporting youth-led initiatives that address community needs and promote sustainable development.

Connecting young leaders with peers, mentors and opportunities across Tanzania and the wider African leadership network.
